# Take Me There

Explore the United States through live webcams. Click any state on the map to watch a real-time feed — from Old Faithful erupting in Wyoming to bears catching salmon in Alaska. (Even birds in Haiwaii!)

**[Open the app →](https://twinkites.github.io/take-me-there)** 

---

## How to Use

**Pick a state**  
Click any state on the map to load a live webcam for that location. States with available cams are highlighted; dimmed states have no feeds yet.

**Browse more views**  
Click **Another view** in the panel to see a different cam from the same state.

**Feeling lucky?**  
Hit the **Random** button in the header to jump to a surprise state.

**Search**  
Use the search box in the header to find cams by state name, park, or keyword — try "bears", "volcano", or "eagle".

**Open full screen**  
Click the **↗** button to open the current feed directly in a new tab.

**Can't see a feed?**  
Some cameras go offline seasonally or are temporarily unavailable. The app will automatically try another cam in the same state. If all cams are unavailable, it will offer a Google Street View of a landmark in that state instead.

## What You'll Find

Over 230 live feeds across all 50 states, including:

- 🐻 Wildlife cams — bears, eagles, manatees, bison, wolves
- 🌋 National park landscapes — Old Faithful, Grand Canyon, Crater Lake
- 🌊 Coastal and ocean cams — Channel Islands, Cape Hatteras, Apostle Islands
- 🦅 Bird feeders and nest cams — Cornell Lab, Decorah Eagles, puffin colonies
- 🏔️ Mountain and weather cams — Mount Rainier, Mount Washington, Glacier
